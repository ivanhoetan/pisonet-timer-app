1. Getting Started

What is the Pisonet Timer System?

It is a system that controls how much time each customer can use a computer, based on money they have paid. Time is tied to the customer's account, not to a specific computer — so a customer can log in on any PC and use their remaining time.

The two apps you will use

AppWho uses itWhat it's for
Admin Portal You (owner / staff) Open on your phone or any computer's web browser. Create customers, add time, see money earned, control the PCs.
Customer PC App Customers Runs on each shop computer. Customers log in here to start their time. It locks the PC when time runs out.

Words you'll see (plain English)

WordWhat it means
Balance / TimeHow much computer time a customer has left.
Top-upAdding time to a customer after they pay.
SessionA customer being logged in and using a PC right now.
StampA loyalty reward. Pay ₱12 in one go = 1 stamp.
OnlineThe customer is currently logged in and playing.
DisabledAn account that is turned off and cannot log in.
The golden rule of pricing: ₱1 = 5 minutes. So ₱12 = 60 minutes = 1 hour.

2. Part A — The Admin Portal

Opening the Admin Portal

  1. On your phone or computer, open a web browser (Chrome, Safari, etc.).
  2. Go to this address:
    https://pisonet-timer-api.onrender.com/admin/
  3. Type your email and password, then tap Log in.
Make it feel like an app on your phone. In Chrome (Android): tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen. On iPhone (Safari): tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Now it opens with one tap, just like a real app.
The first time you open it after the shop has been quiet, it may take up to a minute to wake up. This is normal. Just wait — it will load.

Finding your way around

On a computer, the menu is on the left side. On a phone, tap ☰ Menu at the top to open it. The menu items are:

DashboardSee who's playing right now and today's earnings at a glance.
UsersCreate customers, add/remove time, reset passwords, redeem rewards.
ComputersSee each PC, lock it, shut it down, or set up a new one.

Dashboard

This is your home screen. It shows:

  • Active sessions — how many customers are playing now.
  • Computers in use and how many are online.
  • Revenue today — money earned today.
  • A live list of who is playing, on which PC, and their time left (the timer counts down on screen).

From the live list you can quickly + Time (top-up), − Time (remove time), or Lock a computer.

Users — your customers

This is where you'll spend most of your time. Each customer is one row.

Create a new customer

  1. Tap New user (top right).
  2. Fill in:
    • Username — what they log in with (letters/numbers, no spaces).
    • Nickname (optional) — a friendly name, e.g. "Juan."
    • Password — at least 6 characters.
    • PIN — optional, you can leave it blank.
    • Mobile number — optional.
    • Starting time — optional, in minutes.
  3. Tap Save.
Customers can also create their own account right on the computer's login screen. You then just add their time after they pay.

Add time (top-up) after a customer pays

  1. Find the customer (use the Search box if needed).
  2. Tap + Time.
  3. Enter how many pesos they paid (remember ₱1 = 5 minutes). You can also tap a quick button: ₱1, ₱5, ₱10, ₱12 · 1 stamp, ₱20, ₱50.
  4. Choose the payment method (Cash, GCash, etc.).
  5. Tap Save.
You can add time even while the customer is playing — their on-screen timer goes up immediately.

Remove time (fix a mistake)

If you accidentally added too much, tap − Time, enter the minutes to remove, and confirm. (Balance never goes below zero.)

Reset a forgotten password

  1. Tap Reset PW on the customer's row and confirm.
  2. A short temporary password appears on screen — give it to the customer.
  3. The customer logs in with it, then changes it to their own using Change password in the PC app.
The temporary password is shown only once, and the customer's old password stops working. Copy it before closing the box.

The Balance column (live)

If a customer is online, the balance shows a live countdown with a green dot — it ticks down as they play. If they are offline, it shows their saved wallet time.

Showing or hiding disabled customers

By default the list shows Active only. Use the dropdown next to Search to switch to Disabled only or All statuses.

Computers

Each shop PC appears here with a colored status:

StatusMeaning
ONLINEOn and ready, no one logged in.
IN USEA customer is playing.
OFFLINEThe PC is off or has no internet.
MAINTENANCEUnlocked for repairs (no paid time used).

Buttons on each computer:

  • Pair — get a code to set up the PC app (see Section 6).
  • Lock — log out the customer (their time is saved) and lock the PC.
  • Shut down — turn the PC off remotely.
  • Maintenance — unlock the PC for cleaning/repairs without using paid time.

Transactions, Reports & Loyalty

  • Transactions — every top-up and time change, with totals.
  • Reports — pick a date to see that day's money and usage. Tap Export CSV (open in Excel) or Print / Save PDF.
  • Loyalty — see stamps earned and free hours given out over a date range.

Audit log & Admins

  • Audit log — shows who did what (top-ups, resets, locks, etc.). Useful for checking staff activity.
  • Admins — add another staff login. Tap New admin, enter an email and password.

3. Part B — The Customer PC App

This app runs on every shop computer. It fills the whole screen and keeps the PC locked until a customer with time logs in.

What a customer does to start

  1. On the locked screen, type Username and Password.
  2. Tap Start session.
  3. The timer starts and they can use the computer.
A customer needs time on their account to log in. If they have ₱0 / 0 minutes, they must pay and get a top-up first.

Creating an account at the PC

New customers can tap Create an account on the login screen, enter a username, nickname (optional), and password, then tap Create account. After that, they pay and you add their time from the Admin Portal.

The timer while playing

  • The remaining time is shown on screen.
  • Minimize — shrinks the timer into a small floating clock in the corner so they can use the whole screen. They can drag it around.
  • Logout & save time — ends the session and saves any unused time back to their account for next time.
  • Change password — lets them set a new password (useful after a reset).

When time runs out

The customer is automatically logged out and the PC locks. Any unused time is already saved. They simply pay for more and log in again.

If no one is logged in, the PC shows a 60-second countdown and then shuts down by itself to save power. This is normal.

For staff: opening the PC for repairs

To use a locked computer for cleaning or updates without paying:

  1. Press Ctrl + Alt + Shift + M on the keyboard.
  2. Type the maintenance code you set when the PC was paired.

You can also start/stop maintenance from the Admin Portal → Computers.

4. Everyday Tasks (Quick Recipes)

💵 A customer pays for time

Admin Portal → Users → find them → + Time → enter pesos → Save.

🆕 A brand-new customer

Easiest: have them tap Create an account on the PC. Then Admin Portal → Users → + Time after they pay.
Or create them yourself with New user.

🔑 A customer forgot their password

Admin Portal → Users → Reset PW → give them the temporary password → they log in and set a new one.

🎁 A customer wants to use their free hour

Admin Portal → Users → if they have 5+ stamps, tap Redeem (gives 1 free hour at a time).

🔒 Free up or stop a computer

Admin Portal → Computers → Lock (logs the customer out, saves time) or Shut down.

📊 Closing the shop / end of day

Admin Portal → Reports → pick today → review totals → Print / Save PDF for your records.

🛠️ Need to fix or clean a PC

At the PC press Ctrl + Alt + Shift + M and enter the maintenance code.

5. Loyalty Stamps Explained

The loyalty program rewards regular customers with free time.

RuleDetails
Earning a stamp A customer earns 1 stamp for every ₱12 paid in a single top-up (that's 1 hour). Paying ₱24 at once = 2 stamps.
No partial stamps Smaller payments (like ₱6) do not build toward a stamp. Only a full ₱12 in one payment counts.
Redeeming 5 stamps = 1 free hour. You redeem it for them in Admin Portal → Users → Redeem.
Stacking Free hours can stack. If they have 10 stamps, that's 2 free hours — but you redeem one hour at a time.
Points Each free hour redeemed also gives the customer 1 point, a lifetime count of how many free hours they've claimed (handy for monthly bonuses).
When taking a ₱12 payment, use the ₱12 · 1 stamp quick button so the stamp is given correctly.

Customers can see their current stamps on the computer screen after logging in.

6. Setting Up a New Computer

Do this once per physical PC. It links the computer to your system.

Step 1 — Register it (Admin Portal)

  1. Admin Portal → Computers → Register PC.
  2. Enter a Computer code (e.g. PC02) and a display name.
  3. Tap Register. A short pairing code appears (like 7K2-Q9F).
The pairing code lasts 15 minutes and can be used once. If it expires, tap Pair on that PC's card for a new one.

Step 2 — Enter the code on the PC

  1. Open the Pisonet Timer app on the new PC.
  2. On the Quick pair tab, the server address is already filled in.
  3. Type the pairing code and a maintenance code (a secret word/number for staff repairs).
  4. Tap Save & connect.

The PC connects and shows the login screen. It's ready for customers.

Repeat for each PC, using a different computer code each time (PC02, PC03, …).

7. Troubleshooting & FAQ

ProblemWhat to do
The Admin Portal is slow or blank when I first open it. The server was asleep. Wait up to a minute — it wakes up and loads automatically.
A customer can't log in ("Insufficient balance"). They have no time. Take payment and add time with + Time.
A customer says "account already in use." They're still logged in on another PC. Lock that PC (Computers → Lock) or have them log out there first.
A customer forgot their password. Users → Reset PW, give them the temporary password.
A PC shows OFFLINE in the portal. Check that the PC is on and connected to the internet. It comes back automatically.
I need to use a locked PC for maintenance. Press Ctrl + Alt + Shift + M and enter the maintenance code.
The pairing code didn't work. Codes expire after 15 minutes. Tap Pair on the PC's card for a fresh one.
I added the wrong amount of time. Use − Time on the customer's row to remove minutes.
I can't find a customer in the list. They may be disabled. Change the status dropdown to All statuses.
A PC shut itself down. Normal — an idle PC with no one logged in powers off after 60 seconds. Turn it back on.

Daily checklist

  • ☐ Open the Admin Portal and check the Dashboard.
  • ☐ Make sure all PCs show ONLINE.
  • ☐ Take payments → add time promptly.
  • ☐ Give stamps using the ₱12 button when relevant.
  • ☐ At closing, save the day's Report.
